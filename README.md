## 👨‍💻 About Me & My Passion

I am a **Computer Science student at HIT** driven by an unyielding curiosity about how things work—and more importantly, how they break.

While my journey began in **Full Stack Development**, mastering the art of building complex web applications, I realized that my true passion lies in the **security** of those systems. I found myself asking not just *"Does this code work?"* but *"How can this code be exploited?"*.

This shift in perspective led me to the world of **Cybersecurity**. I believe that **to be a great defender, you must understand the attacker.** My background in software engineering (React, Node.js, Databases) gives me a significant edge in **Application Security (AppSec)**. I don't just run tools; I understand the underlying code, the logic flaws, and the architecture that enables vulnerabilities to exist.

### 🚀 What Drives Me?
* **The Puzzle:** Every vulnerable system is a puzzle waiting to be solved. I love the thrill of the hunt—digging through logs, analyzing packets, and reverse-engineering binaries to find that one critical oversight.
* **The Impact:** In a digital-first world, security is not optional. I am passionate about protecting data and ensuring the integrity of the software we rely on.
* **Continuous Evolution:** Cyber is a field that never sleeps. The constant need to learn new attack vectors, adapt to new technologies, and stay ahead of threats is what keeps me engaged every single day.
